Track 01 Fit — This product is right

Urban Store satisfies both explicit pillars of Track 01, and clears every bar requirement with server-side enforcement rather than prompt instructions:

  1. Revenue growth agent — A data-driven campaign orchestrator with five campaign types and a write-back loop that persists actual units sold, delta-revenue, and a bounded projection-accuracy score to each campaign row.
  2. AI-buyer transactable end-to-end — A 9-tool MCP server for Claude Desktop, a 13-tool Groq in-app chat agent, a semantic catalog with structured product relationships, Razorpay order/HMAC-confirm/webhook, and three identity paths (OAuth PKCE, personal API keys, session cookies).
  3. “Why now” alignment — Implements the convergent primitives of UAP/ACP/AP2/x402 (scoped delegated authority, server YES-gate, per-grant audit, HMAC verification, atomic stock) with honest, documented non-claims where the target protocol has not finalized.

§ 04

Key Differentiators

Where this beats typical hackathon demos
D1

Revenue loop closed (not just projected)

Most AI campaign demos stop at “the AI predicts +₹X”. Urban Store writes measured actual-units, delta-revenue, and a bounded 0…1 projection-accuracy back to each Campaign row. Merchants can see if the AI is over-optimistic over time and tune discount depth in the next round.

D2

Security enforced in code, not in prompts

The MCP YES gate uses a cryptographically-random, subtotal-bound, single-use nonce issued server-side — prompt injection cannot bypass it. Cart policy and rate limits fire in the route layer before any business logic runs.

D3

Honest technical claims

HMAC-SHA256 is explicitly labeled as a symmetric signature primitive; no fabrication of asymmetric ECDSA-based verifiable claims. Judges familiar with AP2/UAP specifics will contrast this favorably with teams that bluff cryptographic primitives.

D4

Three identity paths for AI buyers

An AI buyer can connect via OAuth 2.0 + PKCE (the Claude standard public-client flow), personal API keys in the MCP URL, or session cookies for in-app. All three paths funnel through a single identity resolver, and every AuditLog row carries the resulting agentGrantId.

D5

Idempotency everywhere

createCheckout reuses a pending checkout for the same cart (no duplicate Razorpay orders). confirmCheckout returns the existing order if already paid. The webhook path handles already-paid and checkout-not-found cases idempotently.

Known Gaps & Risk Register

Honest assessment for pitch Q&A
Gap Severity Impact on judging & recommended posture
No scripted Razorpay test-mode walkthrough in deliverables Medium Judges may not believe the Razorpay integration actually functions without a click-through. Record a 60-second curl-or-browser walkthrough and embed it in the pitch deck.
Upsell is driven by static product-relationship fields rather than co-occurrence-trained scoring Low Seed frequentlyBoughtWith arrays are sufficient for a hackathon. The infrastructure for upsell is correct; real co-occurrence training is the natural follow-up.
SpendingMandate & StockReservation are schema-only primitives Medium The columns exist in the database but are not yet wired into checkout enforcement. Frame honestly as “the schema shape is ready for UAP Reserve Pay interop.”
No x402-native 402 Payment Required responses Low Razorpay payment URLs are more practical for real buyers. A one-line alternate response shape on the checkout route is a trivial addition if needed for protocol-demo parity.
Informational-only campaign types (BUNDLE, CROSS_SELL, SEASONAL) Low Only CLEARANCE and URGENCY currently mutate prices. The performance write-back loop works correctly for product-scoped campaigns — the majority. Bundle-level coupon infrastructure is the natural extension.
